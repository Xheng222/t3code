# Archive settled threads

## Behavior

Hovering over an archivable settled thread or moving keyboard focus to its row reveals **Archive thread** to the left of **Un-settle thread**. The action uses the thread menu's archive confirmation setting and offers Undo after archiving.

## Upstream coverage

The settled row offers a direct archive action alongside **Un-settle thread**, respects the archive confirmation setting, and offers Undo.
