# Turn review replay

## Behavior

The Replay (dev) provider offers "Turn review (slow)", which replays one turn with thoughts, reads, a search, commands, an edit, todo updates, and a reply at three times the recorded delays. A scenario's optional `delayMultiplier` scales its replay delays.
